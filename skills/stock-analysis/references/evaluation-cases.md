# Evaluation cases

Run the executable tests for arithmetic, provenance and continuity; separately assess research judgments on supplied evidence. These cases are acceptance prompts, not claims that all sectors have been validated with live company research.

| Case | Evidence to include | Expected invariant |
|---|---|---|
| Mature cash generator | Eight quarters, balance sheet, SBC, buybacks, positive EPS, quote | Business quality separate from valuation; net share change and CAPEX definition explicit. |
| Bank | Capital ratios, credit losses, deposits and regulatory filing | Industrial FCF/net-debt screens inapplicable with sector alternatives. |
| Unprofitable infrastructure builder | Contract milestones, CAPEX, cash/restricted cash, conditional financing | No fabricated P/E; runway conditional; unresolved cause allowed. |
| Cyclical | Peak/trough earnings, inventory, working capital | Low peak-earnings P/E is not an automatic buy/sell. |
| Recent IPO | Short public history, split/share-class changes | Missing five/ten-year history remains missing; no invented CAGR. |
| Incomplete disclosure | Release available, filing/transcript pending | Visibly partial/provisional; no claim of reading missing sources. |
| Paired one-input case | Same packet except currency, period, EPS basis or dilution | Incompatible calculations rejected; changed fundamentals alter only dependent conclusions. |
| Return-history reasoning | 100 five years ago, 50 three years ago, 200 now | +100% versus +300% is mathematically valid. |
| Consecutive periods | Saved criterion plus later actuals | Frozen criteria; every old item gets a precise outcome; replacements apply prospectively. |
| Restatement | Original baseline and revised comparator | Both preserved; explanation of comparability, no silent rewrite. |
| Beginner reading | Full report, all accordions closed, both languages | Six-part question flow; concepts before figures; at most three explained figures per analytical section; practical implications and material risks visible. |
| Editorial simplification | Frozen research packet with technical prose | Clearer authored guide; identical cutoff, facts and watch criteria; original analysis/export retained; no invented research. |
| Reading controls | Guided/Detailed, language/theme and grouped source links | Full details expand without changing conclusions; preferences independent; grouped sources include calculation inputs; raw IDs absent from reading path. |

For an independent behavioral pass, supply only the installed skill, a realistic request, the original frozen baseline and raw new evidence. Use an isolated scratch output directory and prohibit live account mutations for fictional evaluation. Do not reveal expected outcomes or suspected defects to the evaluating agent.
