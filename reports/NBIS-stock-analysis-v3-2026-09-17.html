What would I own?

Nebius rents the powerful computers and software that companies need to build and run AI. It must buy or secure the equipment, electricity and facilities before customers can use them. You own a growing infrastructure operator, with some smaller businesses and investments alongside it.

Why it matters

The advantage must show up in reliable service, customer retention and profit after equipment costs. Access to expensive chips alone is not a lasting competitive advantage.

Competitive advantage is promising, but durable returns and independently verified leadership are not established. Geographic revenue detail is from FY2025.

Explain this

A moat is a reason customers stay even when competitors have money and similar hardware.

Hypothetical example: Hypothetical: a cheaper server saves $10,000, but moving a production system costs $100,000. Integration can protect the incumbent.

Common trap: A temporary shortage can raise prices without creating a lasting moat.

Microsoft/Meta contracts validate the ability to win major customers, but those customers have bargaining power and alternatives.
